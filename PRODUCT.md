# Cartrest

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS

## Stack

SwiftUI, AppKit, SceneKit, Core Graphics, and Image I/O. Cartrest opens from Finder and keeps imported artwork in the local session.

## Users

Makers printing Nintendo DS, 3DS, Switch, and Switch 2 cartridge coasters and applying their own vinyl labels.

## Product Purpose

Prepare artwork to fit the selected cartridge's STL recess and arrange labels on a sheet at full scale.

## Operating Context

A typical setup uses a Creality K2 SE with dark gray PLA for the coaster and a compatible paper printer for printable vinyl.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Switch between four console workspaces. Each tab holds its own images, crops, copy counts, model dimensions, and paper settings during a session. Adjust the coaster width to scale its proportions and export a matching STL. Orbit the 3D coaster to inspect the fitted artwork and rear contact channels. Export a PDF with a vector cut line and 50 mm ruler, or export the selected artwork as a PNG with DPI metadata.

## Brand Commitments

Cartrest is a single English word. The app uses English copy, native macOS controls, and a cutting mat preview measured in millimeters.

## Console contributions

A versioned JSON profile defines the tab label, nominal measurements, artwork palette, and printable construction recipe. Contributors can import a new profile in the app or submit the same JSON to the repository.
