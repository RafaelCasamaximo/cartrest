# Example artwork

Select **Add images…** in Cartrest and choose a fitted cover or cartridge label below. The artwork comes from the North American Nintendo DS releases. The covers are cropped to the front illustration. The cartridge labels are cropped from the front scans and given a small paper margin for the cut. The complete images are included as downloaded.
